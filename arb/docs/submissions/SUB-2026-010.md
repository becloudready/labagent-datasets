# SUB-2026-010 PaymentsEdge card payments API

## Summary

An API for taking card payments in the app and web store, replacing the third-party hosted payment page.

Sponsor: VP Digital Commerce. Lead architect: Tomas Berg. Request type: New system or platform. Tier 3 (Strategic). Data classification: Restricted. Service tier: Tier 1. Estimated cost: 3.3 million US dollars.

## Architecture

- Payment service in us-east-1 that receives card details and forwards them to the payment processor.
- A cache holds recent transactions for dispute handling.
- A single region deployment with nightly backups.

## Non-functional requirements

150 payments per second peak. Availability 99.95 percent. Recovery time 4 hours.

## Standards compliance matrix

| Requirement | Status | Note |
|---|---|---|
| STD-DAT-003 R2 | Deviation | Card numbers are held in the cache without tokenisation. |
| STD-RES-005 R2 | Deviation | A recovery time of 4 hours does not meet the Tier 1 objective of 60 minutes. |
| STD-API-004 R6 | Met | Mutual TLS bound tokens. |

## Options considered

- **Keep the hosted payment page.** No card data in scope but a poor customer experience.
- **Build the payment API in house.** Chosen by the team.

## Decisions

### G2: Not approved

Complete: 2026-05-05. Decided: 2026-05-26. Paused: 3 business days.

## Discussion and rationale

The board did not approve the design. Card numbers must not be stored outside a tokenised vault, and a 4 hour recovery does not meet the Tier 1 objective for a payment service. The team was asked to resubmit.
