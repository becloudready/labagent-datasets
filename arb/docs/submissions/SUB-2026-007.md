# SUB-2026-007 SurveyPulse customer survey tool

## Summary

A SaaS survey tool for post-call satisfaction surveys. Responses do not include account numbers or phone numbers.

Sponsor: Director Customer Insights. Lead architect: Samir Haddad. Request type: SaaS onboarding. Tier 1 (Fast-track). Data classification: Internal. Service tier: Tier 4. Estimated cost: 0.03 million US dollars.

## Architecture

- SaaS product with single sign-on and SCIM provisioning.
- Survey links are generated with a random token, not a customer identifier.
- Results are exported weekly to the data platform.
