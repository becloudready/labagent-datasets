# SUB-2026-002 CareAssist contact-centre generative AI assistant

## Summary

A generative AI assistant that drafts answers for contact-centre agents and, in a second phase, answers customers directly in the chat channel, grounded on the knowledge base and the customer's own plan details.

Sponsor: SVP Customer Care. Lead architect: Aiko Tanaka. Request type: Generative AI use case (customer facing). Tier 3 (Strategic). Data classification: Confidential. Service tier: Tier 2. Estimated cost: 2.8 million US dollars.

## Architecture

- Retrieval-augmented generation over the care knowledge base and plan catalogue, using Amazon Bedrock inside the Meridian tenancy.
- Retrieval is trimmed by the permissions of the signed-in agent or by the customer's own identity.
- Every answer shows its sources. A customer can reach a human agent in one step.
- Prompts and responses are logged with personal data redacted.

## Non-functional requirements

1,800 concurrent chats. Median answer time under 4 seconds. Availability 99.9 percent.

## Options considered

- **Agent assist only.** Lowest risk but delivers about a third of the benefit.
- **Agent assist now, customer chat after a pilot.** Adds the customer channel after evidence is gathered. Recommended.

## Discussion and rationale

The board asked what happens when the assistant has no answer. The team showed refusals in the test set. The Security Architect required a red-team test and a guaranteed handoff to a person.
