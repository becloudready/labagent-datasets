# SUB-2026-001 BillingNext rating engine modernisation

## Summary

Replace the 14-year-old rating engine that prices every call, message, and data session with a cloud-native service, without changing the customer bill format.

Sponsor: VP Customer Billing. Lead architect: Samir Haddad. Request type: Major change to an existing system. Tier 3 (Strategic). Data classification: Restricted. Service tier: Tier 1. Estimated cost: 6.4 million US dollars.

## Architecture

- Stateless rating services on managed Kubernetes in us-east-1, scaled by session volume.
- Usage events arrive on the managed Kafka platform; rated records are written to an encrypted ledger database.
- A warm standby in us-west-2 is kept in sync with asynchronous replication.
- The existing bill-run system consumes rated records through an unchanged interface.

## Non-functional requirements

Peak 42,000 usage events per second. Availability 99.95 percent. Recovery time objective 60 minutes, recovery point objective 5 minutes.

## Standards compliance matrix

| Requirement | Status | Note |
|---|---|---|
| STD-CLD-001 R9 | Deviation | Warm standby, not active-active. Recovery within 60 minutes, not 15. Dispensation DISP-2026-001 requested. |
| STD-RES-005 R2 | Met | Design meets Tier 1 objectives of 60 minutes and 5 minutes. |
| STD-DAT-003 R2 | Met | Ledger encrypted with customer-managed keys, access audited. |
| STD-OBS-006 R4.1 | Met | Logs retained 13 months in the central platform. |
| STD-CLD-001 R10 | Met | FinOps review scheduled before go-live. |

## Options considered

- **Active-active in two regions.** Meets STD-CLD-001 R9 but adds an estimated 2.1 million dollars and 9 months. Rejected on cost and time.
- **Warm standby with a quarterly failover test.** Meets the Tier 1 objectives of STD-RES-005 and costs 0.6 million dollars. Recommended.
