# SUB-2026-005 Customer360 lakehouse

## Summary

A governed lakehouse that joins billing, care, and network data into one customer view for analytics and marketing, replacing three overlapping data marts.

Sponsor: Chief Data Officer. Lead architect: Aiko Tanaka. Request type: New system or platform. Tier 3 (Strategic). Data classification: Restricted. Service tier: Tier 2. Estimated cost: 4.1 million US dollars.

## Architecture

- Object storage with a table format, in us-east-1 with a replica in us-west-2.
- Ingestion from source systems through the managed Kafka platform and scheduled extracts.
- Column-level access control, with Restricted columns masked by default.
- Pipeline and query logs include customer identifiers such as the account number.

## Non-functional requirements

Daily refresh by 06:00. 400 analysts. Recovery time 4 hours, recovery point 1 hour.

## Standards compliance matrix

| Requirement | Status | Note |
|---|---|---|
| STD-DAT-003 R5.2 | Conflict | Logs that contain customer identifiers must be deleted or pseudonymised within 90 days. |
| STD-OBS-006 R4.1 | Conflict | Production logs must be retained for 13 months. The two standards cannot both be met for these logs. |
| STD-DAT-003 R3 | Met | Non-production uses synthetic data. |
| STD-DAT-003 R6 | Met | All storage in the United States. |

## Options considered

- **Keep identifier logs 13 months.** Follows the logging standard but breaks the data standard and the privacy commitment.
- **Pseudonymise identifiers before logs leave the pipeline.** Allows 13 months of pseudonymised logs and 90 days of identifier logs. Recommended.

## Decisions

### G2: Approved with conditions

Complete: 2026-04-07. Decided: 2026-04-28. Paused: 2 business days.

## Discussion and rationale

The submission exposed a conflict: STD-DAT-003 R5.2 requires identifier logs to be gone in 90 days, while STD-OBS-006 R4.1 requires 13 months for production logs. Following the Appeals and Escalation process the Chair ruled for this submission only that identifier logs are kept 90 days and a pseudonymised copy is kept 13 months. The ruling does not change either standard.
