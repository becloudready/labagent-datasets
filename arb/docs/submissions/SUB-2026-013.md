# SUB-2026-013 Legacy CRM decommission

## Summary

Retire the legacy customer relationship management system after migration to the new platform.

Sponsor: VP Customer Care. Lead architect: Aiko Tanaka. Request type: Decommissioning a system that holds Confidential or Restricted data. Tier 1 (Fast-track). Data classification: Confidential. Service tier: Tier 3. Estimated cost: 0.2 million US dollars.

## Architecture

- All records were migrated and reconciled.
- Records under retention are archived to encrypted storage with the original retention schedule.
- The system is switched off after a 30 day read-only period.

## Non-functional requirements

Not applicable.

## Standards compliance matrix

| Requirement | Status | Note |
|---|---|---|
| STD-DAT-003 R4 | Met | Retention schedule applied to the archive. |
| STD-DAT-003 R5.1 | Met | Customer records archived for 7 years after account closure. |
| STD-DAT-003 R7 | Met | Backups follow retention. |

## Options considered

- **Keep the system running read-only.** Costs 180 thousand dollars a year.
- **Archive and switch off.** Recommended.
