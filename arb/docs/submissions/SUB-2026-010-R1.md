# SUB-2026-010-R1 PaymentsEdge card payments API (resubmission)

## Summary

Resubmission of SUB-2026-010 with card data tokenised at the edge by the processor and a two-region warm standby.

Sponsor: VP Digital Commerce. Lead architect: Tomas Berg. Request type: New system or platform. Tier 3 (Strategic). Data classification: Restricted. Service tier: Tier 1. Estimated cost: 3.9 million US dollars.

## Architecture

- Card details go directly from the customer's device to the processor, which returns a token. The Meridian service never sees the card number.
- A warm standby in us-west-2 with a tested failover to meet the Tier 1 objectives.
- The cache holds tokens only.

## Non-functional requirements

150 payments per second peak. Availability 99.95 percent. Recovery time 60 minutes, recovery point 5 minutes.

## Standards compliance matrix

| Requirement | Status | Note |
|---|---|---|
| STD-DAT-003 R2 | Met | Tokenisation removes card numbers from Meridian systems. |
| STD-RES-005 R2 | Met | Warm standby meets 60 minutes and 5 minutes. |
| STD-CLD-001 R9 | Deviation | Warm standby, not active-active. Covered by the precedent in DISP-2026-001 and ruled on at the meeting. |

## Options considered

- **Active-active.** Meets STD-CLD-001 R9 at roughly twice the cost.
- **Warm standby.** Meets STD-RES-005. Recommended.

## Decisions

### G2: Approved with conditions

Complete: 2026-08-04. Decided: 2026-08-25. Paused: 1 business days.

## Discussion and rationale

The resubmission resolved the card data finding. The board noted the same recovery-objective difference between the cloud and resilience standards seen in SUB-2026-001 and applied the same ruling.
