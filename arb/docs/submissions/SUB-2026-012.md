# SUB-2026-012 Observability platform migration

## Summary

Replace the in-house logging and metrics stack with a SaaS observability platform.

Sponsor: VP Platform Engineering. Lead architect: Samir Haddad. Request type: SaaS onboarding. Tier 2 (Standard). Data classification: Confidential. Service tier: Tier 2. Estimated cost: 1.4 million US dollars.

## Architecture

- Agents on every service forward logs, metrics, and traces to the vendor platform.
- The vendor's single sign-on is configured. Automated user provisioning was not yet available.
- Logs are retained 13 months in the vendor platform.

## Non-functional requirements

Ingest 3 terabytes a day. Availability per vendor 99.9 percent.

## Standards compliance matrix

| Requirement | Status | Note |
|---|---|---|
| STD-TPR-008 R4 | Deviation | The vendor did not support SCIM at contract time. Dispensation DISP-2026-007 requested. |
| STD-OBS-006 R4.1 | Met | 13 month retention. |
| STD-TPR-008 R2 | Met | SOC 2 Type II report received. |

## Options considered

- **Keep the in-house stack.** Costs more and lags in features.
- **Move to the SaaS platform.** Recommended.

## Decisions

### G2: Approved with conditions

Complete: 2026-02-10. Decided: 2026-02-24. Paused: 0 business days.

## Discussion and rationale

The missing SCIM support was accepted for six months provided the vendor delivered it.
