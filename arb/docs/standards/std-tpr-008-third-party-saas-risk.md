# STD-TPR-008 Third-Party and SaaS Risk Standard (version 2.0)

## Requirements

- **R1 Assessment before contract.** A security and privacy assessment MUST be completed before a SaaS contract is signed.
- **R2 Assurance.** A supplier that handles Confidential or Restricted data MUST hold a current SOC 2 Type II report or ISO 27001 certificate.
- **R3 Data processing agreement.** A data processing agreement MUST be in place before any customer or employee data is shared.
- **R4 Identity.** SaaS products MUST support single sign-on with the corporate identity provider and automated user provisioning with SCIM.
- **R5 Exit.** The contract MUST include an exit plan and return or deletion of Meridian data within 30 days of termination.
- **R6 Restricted data.** SaaS products that handle Restricted data MUST be reviewed at Gate 2 as Tier 3 submissions.
- **R7 Reassessment.** Suppliers MUST be reassessed every year.
- **R8 Sub-processors.** The supplier MUST disclose its sub-processors and notify Meridian of changes.
