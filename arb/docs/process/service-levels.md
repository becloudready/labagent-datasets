# ARB Service Levels

## Service levels by tier

| Item | Service level |
|---|---|
| Front Door triage of Form ARB-F1 | 2 business days |
| Tier 1 (Fast-track) decision | 3 business days from Complete |
| Tier 2 (Standard) decision | 10 business days from Complete |
| Tier 3 (Strategic) decision | 20 business days from Complete |
| Dispensation request (ARB-F5) | 15 business days from Complete |
| Expedited dispensation request | 5 business days, only with approval of the CTO delegate |
| Appeal (ARB-F6) | 10 business days from the appeal being accepted |
| Publication of minutes | 2 business days after the meeting |

## How the clock works

The service level clock starts when the Front Door Coordinator marks the submission Complete. It counts business days and excludes weekends and company holidays. The clock pauses while the ARB is waiting for information from the requester. The total pause time on one submission is capped at 15 business days. If the requester has not responded by then, the submission is withdrawn and must be resubmitted.

## What is measured

Cycle time is the number of business days from Complete to the decision, excluding paused days. The ARB reports median cycle time per tier each month. The service level is met when the decision is issued within the stated number of business days.

## Expedite

Only dispensation requests can be expedited. Design reviews cannot be expedited: a project that needs speed should use the Tier 1 route where it qualifies, or start earlier.
