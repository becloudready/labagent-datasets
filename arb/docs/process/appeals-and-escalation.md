# Appeals and Escalation

## Appeals

A sponsor who disagrees with an ARB decision may appeal. The appeal (Form ARB-F6) must be filed within 10 business days of the decision and must contain new information that the ARB did not have, or show that the standards were applied incorrectly. A disagreement alone is not grounds for appeal.

Appeals are heard at the monthly full ARB meeting with the Architecture Council. The Chair of the ARB does not vote on an appeal of their own decision. Only one appeal is allowed per decision. The appeal decision is issued within 10 business days of the appeal being accepted and is final, except as set out below.

## Escalation to the CTO

Either the sponsor or the Chair may escalate to the Chief Technology Officer when the business impact of a decision is disputed, when two standards conflict and the ARB cannot resolve it, or when a dispensation cannot be renewed. The CTO decides in writing. The decision, the risk acceptance, and the name of the accountable executive are recorded in the governance log.

## Conflicts between standards

When two architecture standards give different answers to the same question, the Front Door Coordinator records the conflict and the Chair decides which applies to the submission in front of the board. The decision applies only to that submission until the standards owners publish a correction.
