# DISP-2026-001 Dispensation (ARB-F5)

## Request

Submission: SUB-2026-001 BillingNext rating engine modernisation. Standard and requirement: STD-CLD-001 R9. Accountable owner: VP Customer Billing.

Deviation: Warm standby in a second region with a 60 minute recovery instead of active-active with a 15 minute recovery.

Reason: Active-active would add 2.1 million dollars and 9 months. STD-RES-005 sets a 60 minute recovery for Tier 1.

Compensating controls: Asynchronous replication with a 5 minute recovery point. Quarterly failover test.

## Decision

Granted: 2026-03-31. Expiry: 2027-03-30. Status as of 2026-10-15: Active.

## Conditions

| Condition | Owner | Due | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Run a full failover test every quarter and keep the evidence. | Billing Platform Lead | 2026-09-30 | Closed |
| Present a business case for active-active. | VP Customer Billing | 2026-12-31 | Open |
