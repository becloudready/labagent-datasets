# DISP-2026-003 Dispensation (ARB-F5)

## Request

Submission: SUB-2026-004 Fleet IoT telemetry hub. Standard and requirement: STD-API-004 R6. Accountable owner: VP Enterprise Services.

Deviation: Device ingestion uses signed device tokens instead of mutual TLS bound tokens.

Reason: Tracker hardware cannot hold a client certificate.

Compensating controls: Tokens are signed with a hardware-backed key and expire after 15 minutes. Applies to the ingestion path only.

## Decision

Granted: 2026-04-14. Expiry: 2027-04-13. Status as of 2026-10-15: Active.

## Conditions

|---|---|---|---|
