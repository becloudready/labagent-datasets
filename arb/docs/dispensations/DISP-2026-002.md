# DISP-2026-002 Dispensation (ARB-F5)

## Request

Submission: SUB-2026-003 EdgeSense 5G edge analytics. Standard and requirement: STD-OBS-006 R4.1. Accountable owner: VP Network Technology.

Deviation: Raw logs on edge nodes kept for 30 days instead of 13 months.

Reason: Edge nodes have 2 terabytes of storage. Shipping all logs would need about 11 terabytes a month.

Compensating controls: Security events are forwarded to the central platform within 5 minutes and retained there for 13 months.

## Decision

Granted: 2026-05-12. Expiry: 2026-09-30. Status as of 2026-10-15: Expired.

This dispensation expired on 2026-09-30. No renewal request (ARB-F5) was filed. The standard STD-OBS-006 R4.1 applies again and the system is non-compliant until it complies or a renewal is granted.
